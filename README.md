# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis


## Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a seasonal
agricultural performance dataset to understand the factors affecting
crop yield, profitability and resource utilization.

## Objectives

- Analyze seasonal agricultural performance
- Compare crop yield and profitability
- Analyze water usage and water efficiency
- Study environmental factors affecting crop yield
- Identify states achieving higher profit with lower water usage
- Identify states achieving higher yield with smaller farm areas
- Analyze disease and pest risk
- Provide evidence-based agricultural recommendations
  
## Dataset 
The analysis uses the dataset:

seasonal_agriculture_performance_dataset.csv

The dataset contains 4,000 records and 28 original columns. Each record represents agricultural/farm-level information.
The dataset contains 8 states, 10 districts, 8 crop types, 3 agricultural seasons, and 4 irrigation methods represented in the categorical analysis.

## Key Insights

- Kharif showed the strongest overall agricultural performance.
- Water usage showed the strongest relationship with crop yield among
  the environmental factors studied.
- Maharashtra showed strong profitability with comparatively lower
  water usage.
- Efficient utilization of land and water is important for sustainable
  agricultural performance.

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that agricultural performance depends on multiple
factors including season, crop type, water availability, environmental
conditions, land utilization and pest risk. Efficient use of resources
can help improve both agricultural productivity and profitability.
